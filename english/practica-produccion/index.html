How this works (read once) ▾

Anki taught you to recognize these words. This gym trains you to produce them by instinct: retrieve the word from a situation, fast, in a fresh context. Same 5 rungs for every word — you climb from safe to hard.

  1. Write one sentence per rung, in your own scenario (rule: not about Nietzsche / philosophy).
  2. Click Export → your sentences get copied, formatted.
  3. Paste them to your tutor chat → get them corrected.
  4. Paste the corrected version back into the box and tick corrected ✓. It stays saved.

The same neutral word (slow) is shown climbing all 5 rungs, so you see the machinery of each joke-type. Don't copy it — copy the move.
